# Ko Vision — Antigravity Hero Portrait Prompts

Use these in Antigravity 2.0 to generate creative hero concepts for thekovision.com. Pick the direction that feels most like you, then use it to inform your real photo shoot too.

---

## Prompt 1: Editorial Minimal
> Portrait of a young Asian man in his mid-20s, clean groomed look, wearing a simple black crewneck, sitting in a modern minimal workspace with a single large monitor behind him. Soft directional window light from the left. Near-black background (#0D0D0D). Shot on 85mm lens, shallow depth of field. Editorial photography style, no smile, confident calm expression. Color grading: desaturated with warm skin tones, high contrast.

## Prompt 2: Creator at Work
> Cinematic portrait of a young Asian man viewed from a slight angle, focused on a screen that casts a subtle glow on his face. Dark room, single desk lamp. The monitor shows a design in progress (blurred). Mood: late-night creative session, quiet intensity. Color palette: near-black shadows, warm amber/yellow highlights from the screen. Shot on 35mm, environmental portrait style.

## Prompt 3: Street Editorial
> Full-body portrait of a young Asian man standing against a dark concrete wall in an urban setting. Wearing all black — fitted jacket, dark jeans. One hand in pocket, relaxed posture. Shot during blue hour with a single warm streetlight creating a yellow highlight on one side. High fashion editorial style, muted colors except for the warm light. 50mm lens, medium depth of field.

## Prompt 4: Bold Studio
> Studio portrait of a young Asian man against a solid black background. Dramatic single-source lighting from above right, creating strong shadows. Clean skin, minimal styling. Expression: direct eye contact, neutral, self-assured. The only color accent is a thin yellow line of light along one edge of the face/shoulder (practical lighting, not post-processing). High-end portrait photography, 70-200mm lens.

## Prompt 5: Digital Native
> Portrait of a young Asian man in a modern dark workspace surrounded by subtle digital elements — a glowing monitor, keyboard with backlit keys, clean desk setup. Shot through a slight reflection in glass, adding depth. Near-black environment with selective focus on the subject. Color grading: cool shadows, warm yellow accent on the tech elements. Futuristic but grounded, not sci-fi. Documentary portrait style.

---

## How to pick

Run all five, see which one makes you think "that's me." If none of them land perfectly, that's data too — tell me what's wrong with each and I'll rewrite. The goal isn't a final image, it's finding the visual direction before you shoot your real photos.
