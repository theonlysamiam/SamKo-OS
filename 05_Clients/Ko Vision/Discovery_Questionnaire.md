# Ko Vision — Discovery (the anti-paralysis version)

**The rule for this whole document:** we are not deciding who Ko Vision is *forever*. We are deciding a **90-day testable version** you commit to and don't reopen. Every question is pick-one, and every question has a **default** you take if you can't decide in 60 seconds. Subtracting options is the whole game. A wrong-but-chosen answer beats a perfect-but-frozen one — you'll learn more in 90 days of doing than in another 2 years of thinking.

Answer in your blunt internal voice. Chloe will walk you through this one question at a time.

---

### 1. Who do you help? (pick one)
- [x] **Creators / personal brands**
**Locked.** People building their own thing online — people like you.

### 2. What's the ONE thing you lead with? (revised)
- [x] **A creative portfolio — your work, your eye, your capabilities.**
**Locked.** Ko Vision isn't selling a packaged service. It's a showreel — proof that you're a capable creator with taste. The site itself is the product.

### 3. The transformation, one line.
**Locked.** "I help myself go from scattered work-in-progress to a polished portfolio that shows what I can actually do."

### 4. The vibe (pick three words)
**Locked.** Clear · Modern · Trustworthy
The brand stays out of the way and frames the work. Taste shows up in the portfolio, not in the chrome.

### 5. Face or faceless?
- [x] **Name and face front-and-center.**
**Locked.** You are the product. People hire people, not logos.

### 6. The proof you'll build in the next 90 days
- [x] **Own socials as the live case study.**
**Locked.** You become your own first portfolio piece. Everything you post, design, and build is both the work and the proof.

### 7. One-sentence elevator line
**Locked.** "Ko Vision is Samuel Ko's creative portfolio — clear, modern work that shows what a digital creator with taste can actually build."
