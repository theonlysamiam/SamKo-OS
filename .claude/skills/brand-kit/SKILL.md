# Skill: brand-kit

This is the recipe the branding agent (or Sam) follows to build a brand kit. Work top to bottom. Keep it specific and slop-free.

## Inputs to gather first
- Brand name and what they actually sell.
- Their audience in one sentence (who buys, and why).
- Any existing assets: website, Instagram/Facebook, logo. Look at them before deciding.
- Three words for the feeling the brand should give off (e.g. "fast, fresh, local").

If an input is missing, ask Sam one sharp question rather than guessing.

## Steps
1. **Read the brand.** Visit the site/socials. Note what's working and what reads as off-brand. One paragraph.
2. **Color palette.** Pick 1 primary, 1 secondary, 2 neutrals, 1 accent. Give each a hex code and a one-line reason it fits the audience.
3. **Typography.** One heading font, one body font (Google Fonts, free). Say where each is used.
4. **Voice.** Three adjectives, then a 2-sentence "we sound like this" sample and a "we never sound like this" sample.
5. **Logo & usage.** Clear-space rule, minimum size, what not to do (no stretching, no recoloring outside palette).
6. **Do / don't list.** Five do's and five don'ts the design and social agents must follow.

## Output
Save as `05_Clients/<Client>/Brand_Kit.md` (or `01_Agents/.../KoVision_Brand_Kit.md` for Ko Vision). Use the headings above in order. End with a one-line note to Sam: what to review and the single choice you most want him to confirm.

## Remember
The brand kit is a draft until Sam approves it (human gate). The design and social media agents are required to follow it once approved.
