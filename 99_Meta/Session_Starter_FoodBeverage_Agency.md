# Session Starter — Food & Beverage Agency (client work)

**How to use:** Open a fresh Claude Code session in your `SamKo OS` folder. Paste **Jon's agency master prompt first** (it defines the agency's infrastructure and product standards), then paste the block below to run your side — onboarding an F&B client through the social media SOP.

---

Chloe — this is a **food & beverage client** session. Jon's master prompt (above) sets the agency's overall infrastructure and standards; your job is my side: onboarding this F&B client and running their social media through our SOP, leaning on my real edge — **food photography**.

Context to work from:
- We serve F&B businesses — restaurants, cafés, food brands. My differentiator is that I shoot food (dark-set and light-set styles; samples in `05_Clients/Ko Vision/site/images/`).
- Follow the **Social Media Management SOP** at `03_Workflows/Standard/Social_Media_Management_SOP.md`, including its two human gates.
- Delegate to the agents: **branding** (brand kit), **design** (Canva edits), **social-media** (schedule + report). Brand/IG audit uses Apify sparingly (free tier) until the `social-audit` skill exists.
- No AI slop (Jon's standard). Client-facing work is polished or it doesn't ship.

Run it one step at a time, concept first, and tell me how to do each part myself:
1. **Onboard** — create `05_Clients/Active/[Client]/` with `profile.md`: name, cuisine, location, current socials, target diner, goals, budget.
2. **Audit** their brand + Instagram + 2–3 competitors → a score and a short strategy. F&B content pillars to consider: signature dishes, behind-the-scenes/kitchen, specials & events, social proof/reviews, and the story/people. **[HUMAN GATE]** I present it; the client approves before we produce.
3. **Plan a content batch** — and mark where a **photo shoot** fits (my food photography is the hook), plus captions and a posting cadence.
4. **My pass** — Canva edits + copy → **[HUMAN GATE]** Jon or I approve → schedule (Metricool/Blotato) → manage → 2-week results report → decide continue / pivot / add.

End with my minimum viable floor. First question back to me: **what's the client's name and Instagram handle?**
