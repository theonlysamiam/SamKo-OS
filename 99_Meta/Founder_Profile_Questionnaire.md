# Founder Profile — Deep Questionnaire

This is the source document for who Samuel is, how he works, and what the system is for. It feeds `soul.md`, the Chloe (COO) brief, and every agent we build after. Answer in any order. You don't have to finish it in one sitting — one section is a perfectly good "floor" for a day. Be blunt; this is an internal voice doc, not client-facing.

---

## Section 1 — The deeper why & the lines you won't cross
*Fills the open gaps in soul.md. This is the foundation Chloe reasons from.*

1. Freedom and proof are the headline. What's underneath that? When you picture the nomadic life actually working, what does a normal Tuesday look like — where are you, who's around, what are you doing?
2. What are you trying to prove, and to whom? (Yourself, family, a past version of you, someone specific?)
3. What are your non-negotiables — the things you won't compromise even for a great client or a big check? (Kinds of work, kinds of clients, hours, values.)
4. What does "enough" look like? Is $15k/month profit the floor, the target, or a checkpoint on the way to something bigger?

## Section 2 — How you learn (so I teach you the right way)
*This decides how I explain things and hand work to you.*

5. When you pick up something new, what sticks best: reading it, doing it with guidance, or watching it done first?
6. Do you go deep on one thing until you own it, or wide across many things at once?
7. When I teach you a new part of the system, do you want the concept first then the steps, or the steps first and the concept after you've done it?
8. How much do you want to understand under the hood vs. just have it work? (e.g., do you want to know *why* an automation fires, or just that it does?)
9. How hard should I push back on you — a gentle nudge, or real friction when I think you're wrong?

## Section 3 — Growth goals & concrete targets
*Turns the long game into something the system can aim at.*

10. 90-day target: by late September 2026, what specifically is true? (Revenue, clients signed, things shipped, skills owned.)
11. 1-year target: by mid-2026 → mid-2027, what does the operation look like?
12. What skill do you most need to level up to keep pace with your partner, in your own words?
13. What's the one bottleneck right now that, if removed, would unlock the most?

## Section 4 — The partnership & where the handoffs live
*So the system fits the real cadence you already have.*

14. How do you and your partner actually split the day-to-day right now? Walk me through a normal week.
15. Where exactly are the handoffs — what do you pass to him, what does he pass to you, and how (Slack, Notion, calls)?
16. What's his system that's "ready to sell"? What does the offer look like and what's the price?
17. What does he expect from you in the next 30 days to be a true partner, not a junior?

## Section 5 — Chloe, your COO
*Defining how your first agent operates before we write her brief.*

18. What do you want Chloe to own end-to-end vs. always check with you first?
19. How should Chloe talk to you — same blunt internal voice as your notes, or her own personality?
20. What's the first thing you want Chloe to take off your plate this week?
21. How often should Chloe check in — a morning brief, end-of-day recap, only when something needs you?
22. When Chloe and you disagree, what wins — your call always, or should she hold the line on the system you set up on a good day?

## Section 6 — The work (your department: social, marketing, clients, sales)
*Current reality, not the ideal. We optimize from the truth.*

23. Social media: which platforms, for whom (the agency, Ko Vision, clients), and what's the current posting reality?
24. Marketing: how do leads find you today, and which channel do you most want to build?
25. Client management: how many active clients, and where does client info live right now?
26. Sales: walk me through how a deal goes from first contact to signed today. Where does it stall?
27. GHL: who are you setting it up for first, and what do you already know vs. need to learn?
28. Ko Vision (thekovision.com): in one line, who is it for and what do you sell them?

## Section 7 — Tools, memory, and where things live
*So the system plugs into what you already use.*

29. Notion is "memory" and Obsidian is your "second brain" — how do you currently use each, and where's the line between them?
30. Of everything on your plate, what do you want automated first, and what do you want to keep doing by hand?
