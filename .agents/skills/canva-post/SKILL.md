# Skill: canva-post

The recipe the design agent (or Sam) follows to make one on-brand social post. Keep it slop-free and brand-driven.

## Inputs to gather first
- Which brand (read its `Brand_Kit.md`).
- The exact copy/message for the post (headline + any subtext). Don't invent claims.
- Platform and size (Instagram post 1080×1080, story 1080×1920, etc.).
- Any image or asset to include. No stock photos for Ko Vision — real work or Sam only.

If an input is missing, ask Sam one sharp question instead of guessing.

## Steps
1. **Set the canvas** to the right platform size.
2. **Apply the brand:** background and text in the brand palette; headings in the brand heading font, body in the body font. Accent color appears once — a highlight, not a fill everywhere.
3. **Lay it out:** one clear focal point, strong hierarchy (headline > subtext > detail), generous margins. The message reads in under 2 seconds.
4. **Check the rules:** does it obey the brand kit's do/don't list? Fix anything that drifts.
5. **Export** (PNG for posts) and save/link it for review.

## Output & gate
Hand Sam the exported design and a one-line note on what to check. It's a draft until Sam or Jon approves it (human gate). Only approved designs go to the publisher. End with Sam's next floor.
