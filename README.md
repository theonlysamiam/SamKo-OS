# SamKo OS

Welcome to the SamKo Operating System! This is the centralized hub for all your workflows, agents, skills, and client management.

## Directory Overview

- **[00_Inbox](./00_Inbox)**: Quick capture for ideas, unsorted notes, and brain dumps.
- **[01_Agents](./01_Agents)**: Your AI agents, their configurations, instructions, and templates.
- **[02_Skills](./02_Skills)**: Definitions and capabilities of skills you've developed or acquired.
- **[03_Workflows](./03_Workflows)**: Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and special automation workflows.
- **[04_Tools](./04_Tools)**: Automations, scripts, and internal tool documentation.
- **[05_Clients](./05_Clients)**: Client CRM, active projects, profiles, and archives.
- **[88_Templates](./88_Templates)**: Reusable markdown templates to keep your documentation consistent.
- **[99_Meta](./99_Meta)**: System documentation, tags index, and configuration for this workspace.

---

*Tip: Use the templates in `88_Templates` to quickly bootstrap new agents, skills, or clients!*
